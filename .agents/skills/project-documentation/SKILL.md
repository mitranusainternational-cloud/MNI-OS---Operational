# Project documentation

Use when a project change, research result, decision, or verification needs a durable record.

## Workflow

1. Classify the artifact: user/developer docs under app docs; cross-project progress, decisions, roadmap, screenshots under `documentation/`; sourced research under `research/`.
2. Follow the existing template and Indonesian language used by the vault.
3. Include a useful title, date/status properties when appropriate, concise purpose, and working Obsidian links.
4. For research, identify source URL/access date, scope, observed facts, interpretation, recommendation, confidence, and unknowns.
5. For progress, record completed work separately from blocked/pending work and link evidence.
6. For screenshots, use synthetic/redacted information and index route, scenario, branch/commit, date, and verification scope.
7. Link new durable pages from `Home.md` or their area index; avoid duplicating the same explanation across many pages.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat a local file's presence as proof that the behavior was verified.
- Do not convert research into a process decision or SOP without owner validation.
- Do not store secrets, credentials, tokens, or sensitive operational data in Git/vault.
- Preserve local Obsidian application preferences.
